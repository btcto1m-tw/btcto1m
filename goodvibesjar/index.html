關於這個 App · About

好心情罐罐是一個幫你收集快樂的小地方。每天寫下一句好事，投進罐子變成一顆星星；心情低落時打開來，看看自己其實擁有這麼多美好。不是日記、不用註冊、完全離線。

GoodVibesJar is a tiny place to collect your happiness. Write one good thing a day, drop it into your jar as a star, and open it whenever you need a reminder of how much good is already in your life. Not a diary — no sign-up, fully offline.
