English

GoodVibesJar ("the App") is developed by btcto1m LLC. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the App to be a space that belongs entirely to you.

We do not collect any personal data. Everything you create in the App is stored only on your device. This content is never uploaded to any server, and neither we nor any third party can access it.

The App requires no account and no internet connection to use. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

The App uses your device's local notifications to remind you to reflect at the time you choose. This runs entirely on your device and involves no server.

You may export your entries to a file at any time to keep or back them up yourself. Where that file is stored is entirely up to you; we never handle it.

If you delete the App, all entries stored on your device are removed with it.

If this policy changes, we will update this page. For any questions, contact support@btcto1m.com.
